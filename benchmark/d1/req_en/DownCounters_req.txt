The node DownCounter receives an integer Time and a boolean Restart, and returns an integer counter Ctr. At the initial instant, Ctr is Time. After that, if Restart is true, Ctr is reloaded with Time. If Restart is false and the previous Ctr is greater than 0, Ctr is decremented by 1. If Restart is false and the previous Ctr is less than or equal to 0, Ctr keeps its previous value.

The node DownCounters receives two integer inputs Time1 and Time2 and a boolean Restart, and returns two integer counters Ctr1 and Ctr2. It contains two independent instances of DownCounter. Ctr1 is obtained by calling DownCounter(Time1, Restart), and Ctr2 is obtained by calling DownCounter(Time2, Restart). The two counters maintain independent histories, while sharing the same Restart signal for synchronous restart.
