An enumeration type t is defined with three states: A, B, and C. A node n receives four inputs x, y, z, and w, all of type t, and returns a boolean value ok. The logic for calculating ok is to check whether input x is equal to any of A, B, or C to ensure that the value of x is valid. Additionally, the node declares a property requiring the ok condition to be met and declares another property to ensure overlap among x, y, z, and w: that is, x equals one of y, z, or w, or y equals one of z or w.
