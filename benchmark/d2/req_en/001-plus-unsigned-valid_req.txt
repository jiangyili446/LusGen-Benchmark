The node top receives a boolean input reset and returns a boolean output OK. It defines an internal integer variable i. The variable i is a counter: at the initial instant, i is 1; from the next instant onward, i increases by 1 from its previous value on every cycle. OK indicates whether the counter satisfies the nonnegative constraint, and is true when i is greater than or equal to 0. The declared property requires OK to always hold, meaning i must remain nonnegative at all times.
